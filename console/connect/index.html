One line on your site,
and the numbers start.

Paste the snippet into the <head> of duonoxx.com. Pageviews, referrers and enquiries flow straight in.

1 · Add the tracker

Anywhere before </head>. Works on any site — static, Webflow, Next.js, WordPress.

Required
<script defer
  src="https://console.duonoxx.com/track.js"
  data-key="dx_12758b97d7da38b4"></script>

2 · Mark your contact form

Add data-duonox-lead and every submission becomes a lead in Clients.

Recommended
<form data-duonox-lead>
  <input name="name"    placeholder="Your name">
  <input name="email"   placeholder="Email">
  <input name="company" placeholder="Company">
  <textarea name="message"></textarea>
  <button type="submit">Send</button>
</form>

3 · Or post from your server

The same endpoint, for forms you already handle server-side.

Optional
curl -X POST https://console.duonoxx.com/api/collect \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"k":"dx_12758b97d7da38b4","t":"lead",
       "name":"Ada Mensah","email":"ada@studio.com"}'

Custom events

Call this anywhere on the page to record a moment that matters.

duonox.track('pricing_viewed')
duonox.lead({ name: 'Ada', email: 'ada@studio.com' })
